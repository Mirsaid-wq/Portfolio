Here’s a text-style summary about Call of Duty and the company behind it: Call of Duty (CoD) is a highly popular first-person shooter (FPS) video game franchise developed by multiple studios under the umbrella of Activision. The franchise first launched in 2003 with the game Call of Duty, which was set during World War II. Over the years, Call of Duty has expanded into various time periods, from modern warfare to futuristic settings, with multiple sub-series, including Modern Warfare, Black Ops, and Advanced Warfare. The main publisher for Call of Duty is Activision, a major video game publishing company founded in 1979, headquartered in Santa Monica, California. Activision oversees the distribution and marketing of the series, making it one of the best-selling video game franchises of all time.

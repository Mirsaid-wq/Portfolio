Call of Duty: Warzone

Call of Duty: Warzone is a free-to-play battle royale game developed by Infinity Ward and Raven Software, released in March 2020. Available on PlayStation, Xbox, and Windows.

Key Features

  • Free-to-play on multiple platforms
  • Multiple modes: Battle Royale, Plunder, Resurgence
  • Dynamic maps like Verdansk and the upcoming Avalon

Recent Updates

Season 01 (2025) introduces a new map, Area 99, and integration with Black Ops 6. Verdansk returns in spring 2025!

Gameplay

Survive the shrinking play area and fight to be the last player or squad remaining. Use Gas Masks to survive toxic gas zones.

Survival Mechanics

Stay ahead of the gas and strategize with the Gas Mask for extended survival in hazardous zones.

Call of Duty: Mobile

Call of Duty: Mobile brings the best of the Call of Duty franchise to your fingertips, with fast-paced action and immersive gameplay.

Game Features

  • Multiplayer and Battle Royale Modes
  • Variety of classic maps and weapons
  • Seasonal updates and new content

Recent Updates

New maps, weapons, and Battle Pass content are available every season, enhancing the mobile experience.

Gameplay

Fight in iconic maps such as Nuketown and Battle Royale modes, with intuitive controls designed for mobile devices.

Survival Mechanics

In Battle Royale, be the last one standing by managing resources, weapons, and battling your opponents.

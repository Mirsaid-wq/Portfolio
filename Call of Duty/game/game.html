Call of Duty: Warzone

Call of Duty: Warzone is a free-to-play battle royale game developed by Infinity Ward and Raven Software. Available on PlayStation, Xbox, and Windows.

Key Features

  • Free-to-play on multiple platforms
  • Multiple modes: Battle Royale, Plunder, Resurgence
  • Dynamic maps like Verdansk and Avalon

Recent Updates

Season 01 (2025) introduces a new map, Area 99, and integration with Black Ops 6. Verdansk returns in spring 2025!
